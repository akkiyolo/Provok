While Rust offers theoretical advantages, the practical reality for ML engineers is different. Python's ecosystem dominance — PyTorch, JAX, TensorFlow — represents decades of optimization and community investment that Rust cannot replicate in the near term.

The learning curve of Rust's ownership model is significant for ML practitioners whose expertise lies in mathematics and model architecture, not systems programming. The opportunity cost of learning Rust could be better spent deepening ML expertise.
